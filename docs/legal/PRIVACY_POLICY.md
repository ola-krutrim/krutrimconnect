## Definitions

The following definitions are used in this policy. In case of any conflict with respect to interpretation of these definitions between applicable law and these terms, interpretation provided under the applicable law shall prevail.

Anonymous data or anonymized data: Data not identifiable to a natural person.

Automated decision making: Making a decision by automated means through algorithms without human involvement.

Customer Data: Any data, information, content, files, datasets, Personal Data, prompts, inputs, outputs, models, applications, configurations, metadata and other materials submitted, uploaded, stored, transmitted or otherwise processed by or on behalf of a customer through the Services, but excludes Service Usage Data, telemetry and other information generated by Krutrim independently in connection with the operation, security, billing and improvement of the Services, provided that such exclusion shall not apply to Personal Data contained in Customer Data or information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to a customer or an identifiable individual merely because it is generated or processed in connection with the Services. Krutrim's use of Customer Data for model training or improvement shall be governed by the Customer Data and AI Processing section below.

Consent: Any freely given, informed, and unambiguous statement which may be given by a clear affirmative action.

Cookies: Small pieces of data given to a web browser by a web server.

Data Fiduciary: A natural or legal person that determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data.

Data Processor: The natural or legal person who processes personal data on behalf of the data fiduciary.

Data Principal: The natural person who interacts with us and whose personal data we collect.

Erasure or Deletion: The irretrievable obliteration or destruction of saved personal data.

Personal Data: Any information identified or identifiable to a natural person.

Personal Data Breach: Any breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to Personal Data processed by Krutrim.

Processing: Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, recording, structuring, alteration, use, disclosure, and analysis.

Pseudonymization: A processing technique where the processed data is not identifiable to a natural person unless coupled with other information.

Third Party: A natural or legal person, public authority, agency, or body other than the data principal, data fiduciary, or processor.

Security Incident: Any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorised access to, acquisition, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction or compromise of Customer Data or any event that materially compromises the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Customer Data or the Services.

Service Usage Data: Technical and operational information generated through your use of the Services, such as usage volumes, resource consumption, configuration and performance information, feature usage, access times, system logs and diagnostics, and other telemetry relating to the operation, security and performance of the Services, excluding Customer Data and Personal Data contained in Customer Data.
